Study sheet

The three forms and an example sentence, 10 verbs at a time.

01

eat

Group 2
Dictionary Formたべるtaberu
Masu Formたべますtabemasu
Te Formたべてtabete

Example

I eat bread for breakfast every day.

まいにち　あさごはんに　パンを　たべます。

Mainichi, asagohan ni pan o tabemasu.

02

Group 1

Masu Formのみますnomimasu
Te Formのんでnonde

Kinou, kafe de tomodachi to koohii o nomimashita.
